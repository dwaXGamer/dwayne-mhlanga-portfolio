who I am

dwaX.

I'm Dwayne Mhlanga - known online as dwaX or dwaXGamer. I'm a cybersecurity enthusiast and developer from Harare, Zimbabwe, currently pursuing a BSc in Cybersecurity & Forensic Auditing at the University of Zimbabwe.

Before university I completed a National Diploma in Information Technology, where I built a strong foundation in web development, systems design, and IT support. That hands-on background is what I bring into my security work - I don't just study attacks theoretically, I've already built the systems that need defending.

My path into security came through building - I noticed the systems I was creating had attack surfaces nobody was thinking about. That curiosity pushed me toward ethical hacking, vulnerability research, and eventually building CipherNest.

I hold the unusual combination of being both a developer and a designer, which means I think about security holistically: not just the backend and the network, but the entire user-facing surface that attackers target through social engineering and UI deception.

Beyond tech, I run Geeked for Christ, a blog where faith and technology meet. I believe integrity and purpose belong in everything you build.
