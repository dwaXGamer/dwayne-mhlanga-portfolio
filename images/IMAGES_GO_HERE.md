# images/

## ⭐ Your logo (nav bar + browser tab icon)

Save your logo here as:
    logo.png

It's used in two places automatically:
- The navigation bar (top-left, next to the dwayne@ubuntu text)
- The browser tab icon (favicon)

Tips:
- A square-ish PNG with a transparent background works best.
- Around 128×128px or larger is plenty. It'll be scaled down.
- If you name it something other than logo.png, update the two <link rel="icon">
  tags in the <head> of index.html and blog.html, and the <img src> in the nav.
